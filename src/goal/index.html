Complexity is not mastery

What my three stages mean

The stage labels describe the complexity and number of evidence sources in the exercises. They do not describe my current ability. A beginner lab can still require careful reasoning, and an advanced lab remains planned until I have done the work and passed the same evidence and review gates.

Claim boundary

I am publishing a learning intention and a route through existing practice materials. I am not claiming that I have completed these labs, mastered their topics or used these methods in a professional role.

One status at a time

How I will describe progress

  1. Planned: I have selected the lab and its prerequisites, but I have not claimed an attempt.
  2. Guided: I have worked with the instructions, examples or prompts and recorded that help.
  3. Solo: I have preserved a first answer to a changed case before checking a reference.
  4. Reviewed: A person has challenged my evidence and reasoning, and I have retained the feedback and correction.
  5. Recall: I have tried different facts later without reusing my earlier answer.

Every lab below starts at Planned. A tool reporting a match does not move the status by itself.

Before I move forward

My review gate

Before I start a lab that depends on earlier work, I will keep a reviewed or equivalent evidence set for each named dependency. For every lab I will preserve the case, first answer, commands or method, exact evidence references, conclusion, uncertainty, help used, correction and next check. If a reviewer is unavailable, I will mark the work as awaiting review and will not call it independently demonstrated.

Public claim limits

Scope and limits

I will use fictional or dummy evidence and the safe boundaries written in each lab. This route does not authorise work on real systems, accounts or data. Helper checks can compare formats and expected decisions, but they cannot judge the quality of my reasoning or prove independent skill. The route is a plan, not a credential, job history or completion record.
