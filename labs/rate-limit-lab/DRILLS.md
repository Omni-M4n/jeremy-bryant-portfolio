# Practice stages

1. Guided: prepare `guided`, write the prediction first, then run it. Keep
   the first notes and compare with WORKED-EXAMPLE.md only afterwards.
2. Prompts: Does remaining 0 mean the current request failed? Does a rejected request restart the clock?
3. Independent: Use `challenge`. Predict how many requests are allowed before and after the wait. Then explain the timed evidence. Keep the reference closed until you have done this.
4. Changed: Use `changed`. Decide which routes affect the count. In a fresh plan, move a single `other` step between two ticket requests and predict again.
5. Teach-back: Explain the threshold and reset to someone who is waiting at a ticket desk.
6. Later recall: next week, use a new attempt folder. Draw a two-second timeline. Show three accepted requests, one later request, and a request after reset.

Label notes `first attempt`, `revised after evidence` or `later recall`.
These labels show the stage. They do not prove skill. Keep what you saw
apart from what you first thought would happen. Ask someone to review your
reasons and what you still need to check.
