# Practice and recall

1. Guided: predict `p1`, then check the worked example. Keep your first answer.
2. With prompts: copy the prepared case and change only `install_exit` to 1.
   Predict the label again. Which rule settles this result?
3. Independent: prepare `challenge`. Keep its reference closed. State what happened
   to the update and what happened to recovery as two separate facts.
4. Changed case: prepare `changed`. Compare the evidence with each rule in order.
   Choose a result and name the next read-only check.
5. Local practice: predict each greeting, then run both toy updates. Inspect both
   receipts and confirm the temporary folders were removed.
6. Teach back: explain a rollback using a saved draft of a school note. Why do you
   still read the restored draft after checking its fingerprint?
7. Later recall: in two days, list the evidence needed to support "update worked"
   and "rollback worked". Name one limitation of a single smoke check.

Handover: preserve first answers, source evidence, version reads, function checks,
rollback proof, help used and any unrun VM step. A reviewer checks your reasoning.
