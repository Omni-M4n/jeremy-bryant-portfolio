# Worked example: record set A

Read this after saving your first attempt.

On 15 January, i1 is still current. Its source is direct. It has confidence 80,
single-host scope and no conflict flag. Use context-only-use. Seven days later
is 22 January. The claim expires first, on 20 January, so review on **20 January**.

i2 reports 95 confidence but is copied. Use seek-origin and review now, on
**15 January**. A high score cannot replace the source evidence.
Reject this weak claim: "95 means relay.test is definitely harmful and must be
blocked everywhere." Ask for report r2's original source. Ask what was seen and
where it was seen. Seek any evidence that conflicts with the claim. The local
labels guide a review. They do not prove danger.

Write the evidence separately from your interpretation. State one thing these
records cannot establish. Keep the first answer and the corrected answer.
