# Practice ladder

1. **Guided, 10 minutes.** Prepare guided as attempt-01. Follow the README's
   tune, choose and holdout commands in that order. Keep the full case closed.
   Save tune counts and your first choice before viewing holdout rows.
   Open WORKED-EXAMPLE.md only after you count the holdout results.
2. **With prompts, 10 minutes.** Put each visible row in one of four cells.
   State the total used under each fraction. Use tune rows to choose a rule.
3. **Independent, 15 minutes.** Prepare challenge as attempt-03. Keep the worked
   example, full case and reference folder closed. View only tune rows first.
   Save your choice and reason, then reveal holdout rows. Keep the choice fixed.
   Predict each count and cite row IDs before running analyse. Note any help.
4. **Changed case, 10 minutes.** Prepare changed as attempt-02. Use the same
   tune, choose and holdout order. Explain how changed costs affect your choice.
   If you later edit a case, use a new copy. It is no longer an unseen test.
5. **Teach back, 3 minutes.** Explain why no false alarms among two routine
   examples cannot prove a real-world rate of zero. Use an everyday example,
   then a row ID. Explain why you must choose before seeing holdout results.
6. **Later recall, 10 minutes.** After two days, recall the rules without help.
   Repeat a count by hand. Mark the case as seen before; a delay does not make
   it unseen. Ask a reviewer for a fresh case before claiming an unseen check.

Journal: prediction; command; observed facts; meaning; unknowns; next check;
help; later recall. A label match is not proof of skill.
