# Guided worked example

Read this after saving your first attempt. This is a fictional example, not a
claim that a learner or an author ran a real engagement.

Item-1 is a staff-approved confirmed fact. Item-2 is a technical hypothesis and must not enter the staff notice or become a breach claim. Item-3 is a staff action owned by the response lead. The context limits the draft recipients to the three reporting staff. Example staff draft: Three staff have reported repeated sign-in prompts. If you see an unexpected prompt, report it through the service desk route you already use, shown here as service-desk.test. The response lead owns this action. The next update is scheduled for 10:30. Draft for the reporting staff only. NOT SENT.

The technical handover can label item-2 as a hypothesis and request a check; it cannot claim that check already happened. The manager section records that no manager-approved effect statement or decision is supplied and requests one from the response lead. If another case lacks a staff-approved fact, staff action or recipient scope, use the README's hold procedure. A hold is preferable to making up a fact or broadening the recipients.
