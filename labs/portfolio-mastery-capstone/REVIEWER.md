# Reviewer sheet

Use this process for an independent attempt. It does not certify mastery.
Do not fill in results for the learner.

1. Check the README prerequisites against saved work. This includes an independently reviewed track, the required local finding report and the redaction review. Check completed work from each track you will combine. Reading a guide alone is not completion.
2. Check that the learner may use the chosen offline evidence. Use fictional records with no live targets. Build a small fresh packet from two or three completed tracks.
3. Change the reasoning, not just names. For example, pair a successful name lookup with a page that will not load. Or show a refused non-owner request with a broken owner request. Supply enough raw facts to settle one issue. Leave a real evidence gap for another. Keep answers out of names, headings and IDs.
4. Write your expected reasons and accepted alternatives before the attempt. Keep this key separate. State the scope, time limit and allowed references.
5. Ask for a dated first attempt, exact evidence references, one next action and its reason. Keep `first-attempt.md` unchanged. Record every hint in `help-log.md`.
6. Ask: Which fact supports your claim? What does it not prove? What would change your mind? What permitted test should come next?
7. Use the rubric below. A missing item is a learning need. Do not invent evidence to fill it. Save comments in `review.md` and changes in `corrections.md`.
8. After 2-7 days, give a short changed observation with no worked example. Save `recall.md`, the date, result and all help used. Leave these blank until the learner tries it.

| Review area | Evidence to look for | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Work stayed inside the agreed offline case | unassessed |
| Reasoning | Claims follow from facts; alternatives are considered | unassessed |
| Evidence | Exact references and untouched first attempt | unassessed |
| Limits | Unknowns and unrun checks stated plainly | unassessed |
| Next action | Small, useful, authorised check with a reason | unassessed |
| Explanation | Learner can explain without reading the answer | unassessed |
| Help | Hints and sources recorded honestly | unassessed |
| Recall | Later attempt recorded separately | unassessed |

Use `observed`, `needs work` or `unassessed` for each row. Give a reason.
Do not let an average score hide missing evidence. Public excerpts need a
separate privacy review and the learner's permission. Leave raw evidence and
private mappings out. A reviewer may accept another answer when the evidence
supports it. Record the reasons.
