# Case packet: the loan cupboard

**Status:** fictional; paper-only; case date 25 October 2026. The owner role,
service desk and records below are invented for practice. No device or person
may be contacted or tested under this packet.

## Written scope

The learner may read `fixtures/guided.json` and its embedded `risk-notes.txt`,
write a local worksheet, and run the offline helper. The learner may propose
controls and questions. The learner may not inspect a real laptop, contact a
visitor, send a service-desk report, change a setting or approve an exception.
The fictional device owner must decide on any real response. No approval record
for a real system is supplied.

## Evidence index

| ID | Supplied fact | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | One scheduled laptop handover occurred beside a public waiting area; visitors could approach. | No repeated unattended handovers are documented. |
| G2 | The inventory lists an offline file with addresses and support needs. | Lock, encryption and access to the file at loss time are unknown. |
| G3 | Locked storage, checkout, reporting, encryption, automatic lock and cache reduction are proposed. | No implementation or effectiveness check is supplied. |
| G4 | A visitor waited outside a closed badge door; reception normally admits booked visitors. | No unauthorized entry is documented. |
| G5 | The room holds the only paper shift rota for a two-person team. | No owner acceptance or new control check is supplied. |

The exact source wording is in `fixtures/guided.json`. Use that file if a
summary here appears ambiguous. The case's `as_of` date is 25 October 2026;
the two fixture review dates are 1 November 2026. Assess the teaching labels
as of the case date, then state that the dates are now historical if you use
the packet later. Do not turn the case into a claim about a present device.

## Decisions requested

- State the event and plausible harm for each risk, with evidence IDs.
- Apply the lab's 1–3 likelihood and impact rubric, then explain the score.
- Name the proposed response and owner role. Distinguish planned controls
  from checked controls and projected residual risk from observed risk change.
- Record the visitor-risk acceptance as **pending**, because the fixture says
  `acceptance: false`. Name the missing acceptance authority and risk threshold.
- Draft a short report that someone could act on without exposing personal
  details or claiming a confirmed data breach.

Missing risk appetite, legal obligations, owner sign-off and technical checks
must be written as **not supplied**. No external source can fill a missing case
fact. A framework category can help organize a question, but it cannot supply
an approval or prove a control.
