# Reviewer guide and changed-case challenge

This guide helps a person assess the reasoning in a completed GRC worksheet.
The supplied guided case and reference are rehearsal. Do not mark independent
completion from a copied reference, a matching helper label, or a prepared
answer.

## Review gates

| Gate | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | The draft names permitted paper work, excluded real-world actions, owner and stop condition. |
| Traceability | Each material claim points to G1–G5 or a precise fixture field; absent facts are marked **not supplied**. |
| Risk judgement | Event, harm, likelihood and impact reasons are separate; the local teaching score is not presented as a measured probability. |
| Control evidence | Proposed controls and checked operation are distinguished; projected residual risk is not called verified. |
| Authority | The visitor acceptance remains pending until a named authorized role and record exist. |
| Communication | The report states what is observed, inferred and unverified without private detail or a false breach claim. |
| Challenge response | The alternative interpretation is tested against source evidence, and the learner explains any change to the first decision. |

Mark each gate **met**, **needs revision**, or **not observed**, with one
evidence reference. Record the reviewer's name or role, date, case version,
questions asked and learner's answer. Preserve the first draft and the review
notes. Only a person may assign an independent-assessment status.

## Changed-case rehearsal

Privately give the learner this paper-only variation after their guided draft:
"On the next day, the workroom door is reported propped open during two
visitor arrivals. No entry or rota loss is confirmed." Ask which likelihood
reason changes, what additional evidence is needed, and whether the previous
pending acceptance remains defensible. A useful answer notes that the new
opportunity is stronger than G4's original closed-door account, but does not
invent an entry or loss. The reviewer should ask how the answer would change
if the report of the open door were later disproved.

This published variation is still rehearsal. For an independent assessment,
the reviewer must supply an unseen authorized or fictional case with changed
facts, keep its answer key private, and assess a first attempt before hints.
The learner then repeats a short recall task on a later day.
